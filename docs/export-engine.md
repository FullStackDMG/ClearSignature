# Accessibility

## 🎯 Goal

Ensure that both:
1. The ClearSignature application
2. The generated email signatures

follow accessibility best practices aligned with WCAG 2.2 AA.

---

## 🧪 Accessibility Checks

### Color Contrast
- Minimum ratio: 4.5:1 for normal text
- Evaluate foreground vs background colors

---

### Images
- All images MUST include alt text
- Decorative images should be flagged

---

### Links
- Must be descriptive
- Avoid "click here" or vague text

---

### Typography
- Minimum readable font size (recommended: 12–14px)
- Use accessible font families

---

### Structure
- Avoid image-only signatures
- Maintain readable hierarchy

---

## 📊 Accessibility Status

- ✅ Pass
- ⚠️ Pass with warnings
- ❌ Needs review

---

## ⚠️ Limitations

- Email clients may override styles
- Full WCAG compliance cannot be guaranteed across all platforms
