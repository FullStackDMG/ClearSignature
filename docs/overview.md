# ClearSignature Overview

ClearSignature is an accessibility-first email signature generator designed to produce WCAG-aware, cross-client compatible email signatures.

The application allows users to create professional email signatures without requiring an account and without storing personal data.

---

## 🎯 Goals

- Provide accessible email signature generation
- Ensure compatibility across major email clients
- Maintain a privacy-first experience (no personal data storage)
- Enable reuse of company and department branding assets
- Offer clear validation of accessibility risks

---

## 👥 Target Users

- Independent contractors
- Small businesses
- Enterprise teams
- Government organizations

---

## 🔑 Core Principles

- Accessibility-first (WCAG 2.2 AA target for app)
- Privacy by design
- Email-client compatibility over visual complexity
- Mobile-first user experience
- Simplicity with professional output

---

## 🚀 MVP Scope

- Single locked signature template
- Required + optional fields
- Logo upload and reuse system
- Accessibility checker
- Dark mode preview
- Export (HTML, plain text, copy/paste)
